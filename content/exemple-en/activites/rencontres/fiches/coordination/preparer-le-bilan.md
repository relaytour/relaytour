## Goal

The Coordination department gathers feedback from each area after the edition and updates the how-to sheets. This review lets the next edition build on the experience gained.

## When

Between J+7 and J+30. Plan a two-hour meeting and a week of writing.

## Steps

1. **J+7.** The department asks each area for three successes, three difficulties, and one proposal.
2. **J+14.** The department leads the review meeting. Each area presents its feedback in five minutes.
3. **J+21.** Each area updates its how-to sheets in the app: the “Common pitfalls” section receives the difficulties of the edition.
4. **J+30.** The department reviews the edited sheets and exports the content to the organization’s repository.

## Contacts

- Coordinators of each area: feedback and sheet updates.
- The association’s board: decisions on the proposals.

## Common pitfalls

- A review done more than two months after the edition loses the useful details.
- Feedback that stays in meeting minutes does not help the next edition: it must go into a sheet.
