## Goal

Every area handles lists of people: registrations, volunteers, partner contacts. This sheet sets the common rules so that these lists do not leave the association’s tools.

## When

Read it before creating the first list of the edition, then each time a list is shared.

## Steps

1. The area stores every list of people in the association’s file space, never in a personal messaging app.
2. The area shares a list only with the people who need it, for the duration of the edition.
3. The area writes contacts as roles in the how-to sheets. A personal address or number never goes into a sheet.
4. After the edition, the area deletes the working lists. Only the review documents remain, without names.
5. A person who asks for their data to be deleted receives an answer within a month. The Coordination department keeps the register of these requests.

## Contacts

- Coordination department: register of processing activities and requests from individuals.
- The association’s board: data controller.

## Common pitfalls

- A registration file exported to a personal spreadsheet often stays on a computer for years.
- A messaging group shows the number of each member to all the others.
