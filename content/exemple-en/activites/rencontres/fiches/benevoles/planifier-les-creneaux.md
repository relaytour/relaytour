## Goal

The Volunteers department builds an attendance table for each site. For each position and each slot, the table shows the number of people expected and their first names.

## When

The department prepares the positions and standard slots at J-150. It opens volunteer sign-up at J-120 and fills in the table at J-60. It confirms attendance at J-21 and sends the schedule at J-10.

Building the table takes about half a day. The department then spends about an hour a week on updates until the edition.

## Steps

1. **J-150.** The department lists the sites, days, and times of each sport.
2. **J-150.** For each site, the department divides the day into slots of 2 or 3 hours.
3. **J-150.** The department puts the positions in rows and the slots in columns. Each cell shows the number of people expected.
4. **J-120.** The department opens volunteer sign-up in a tool of the association.
5. **J-60.** The department enters the first names in the table. It checks that no person registered for a sport holds a position during their matches.
6. **J-60.** The department limits each volunteer to two consecutive slots and plans a break for the meal.
7. **J-21.** The department confirms each person’s attendance and fills empty cells with a reserve of available people.
8. **J-10.** The department sends each volunteer their slots, the venue, and the arrival time.
9. **J+0.** The host at each site checks off each arrival and reports absences to the department.
10. **J+14.** The department counts the hours of attendance for the thank-you messages and the review.

## Contacts

- Coordinators of the sports: times, matches, and needs by site.
- Logistics department: setup and teardown needs.
- The association’s board: reserve volunteers.

## Common pitfalls

- Without a table by slot, a position can stay empty and nobody notices before the day itself.
- Volunteers tire quickly in a slot longer than 3 hours.
- In a personal messaging group, every member sees the phone numbers of all the other people. The department uses the association’s tools.
