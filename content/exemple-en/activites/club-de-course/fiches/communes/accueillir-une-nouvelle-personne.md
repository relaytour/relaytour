## Goal

Every person who joins the club during the season finds a group at their level and a contact person from their first session.

## When

At each arrival, throughout the season.

## Who

The coordinators of the group that receives the person. The Club life area keeps the list of people to welcome.

## Steps

1. The Club life area sends the group the person’s first name and stated level, through the club’s mail account.
2. A coordinator of the group introduces the session, the warm-up, and the route.
3. After three sessions, the group offers the person to stay or to change groups.

## Points of attention

- The list of people to welcome stays in the club’s tools. It does not circulate through personal messaging apps.
- A person can change groups at any time during the season.

## Resources

The shared mailbox contact@exemple.org receives questions from interested people.
