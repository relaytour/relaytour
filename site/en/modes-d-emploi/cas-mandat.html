Use case · The whole team

Support a committee through its term

A committee works without an event day, at the pace of its meetings. This use case shows how Relaytour keeps track of the work between meetings and from one term to the next.

This use case shows one path among others. Each step points to the user guide that describes the screen in detail.

The case

Six people, three projects and two years

The board of an association entrusts the welcome of new members to a committee of six people, for a two-year term. The committee meets once a month.

It runs three projects: a welcome guide, a mentoring scheme and an open day. Most of the work moves forward between meetings.

Before you start

What you set up

An organization admin creates the committee’s activity. An activity admin then sets its kind, its groups, its phases and its modules.

An activity of the “Instance” kind

/<activity>/admin/activites
  • “Instance” kindAn instance gathers a board, a council or a committee. Its period is called a “term” on every screen.
  • One area per projectThe welcome guide, the mentoring scheme and the open day become three areas of a “Projects” group. A small committee can start with a single area.
  • Calendar moduleIt holds the meetings, the meeting notices and the date polls.
  • PhasesThe phases can follow the stages of the term: assessment, proposals, implementation, review.
  • Open to interestsMembers choose the projects they are interested in from “All areas”.

The activity admin guide describes these settings in its “Activities” section.

The term step by step

Five stages, from launch to handover

1. The launch

The activity admin creates the two-year term, then adds each member by their address. The admin then appoints the members to the projects of their choice.

People · Team

2. The meeting rhythm

The admin places one meeting per month in the global calendar and calls the whole team. Each meeting becomes a team check-in. A date poll settles the slot when the time changes.

Global calendar · Calendar

3. Between two meetings

Each decision becomes a task of the project concerned, with a due date and an assigned person. My space ranks these tasks by priority until the next team check-in.

My space · An area

4. The record of the work

Each file the committee studies gets a sheet: the findings, the decisions, the role contacts. The “Also concerns” list makes a sheet readable from the other projects.

A sheet · Also concerns

5. The end of the term

The admin archives the term and opens a new one, which carries over the recurring tasks. The new team then receives its appointments. The sheets remain and welcome the people who arrive.

Editions, seasons, or terms

A meeting that prepares the next one

/<activity>/agenda

A meeting links up to twenty tasks of the period. These tasks form the agenda: each person sees before the meeting where each item stands. The day before the team check-in, the priorities email reminds everyone of their upcoming tasks.

  • Team check-inCheck this box in the window of a global meeting. It is checked automatically when the meeting calls the whole team.
  • CommentWrite in the thread of a task the minutes of what was decided about it.
  • Schedule a meetingCreate a meeting from a task, with the task already linked.

Each person sets the priorities email in their preferences: their areas, the whole activity, or no email.

Good practices

Three habits for long-term work

Each meeting produces dated tasks

A decision without a due date does not enter the priorities. Before closing a meeting, the team gives each decision a task, a due date and a person.

The sheet rather than the email

Minutes sent by email get lost in inboxes. A sheet can be reread, keeps its versions and remains available to the team of the next term.

A team check-in at each meeting

The priorities cover the days until the next team check-in. A monthly meeting marked as a team check-in aligns these priorities with the real pace of the committee.

Keep it simple

What you do not need

The schedule is useful only if the committee holds dated interventions, for example a stand during an event of the organization. The “Volunteers” and “Ranking” modules stay off.

A committee fits in three screens: My space, Calendar and Resources.
